To analyze the impact of AI displacing mid-skill knowledge workers on municipal and state tax bases, we can break down the problem into several components and model the effects over time. Here's a structured approach:

### 1. Initial Tax Revenue Loss
- **Employment Contraction**: Assume an 8% sustained contraction in mid-skill employment.
- **Tax Revenue Impact**: If the initial tax revenue is \( X \), the loss in the first year is \( 0.08X \).

### 2. Compounding Tax Loss Over Years
- **Yearly Contraction**: Each subsequent year's loss is 8% of the reduced tax base.
- **Formula**: 
  \[
  \text{Loss in Year } n = 0.08 \times (X - \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} 0.08^i X)
  \]
- **Total Loss Over \( N \) Years**:
  \[
  \text{Total Loss} = X \times (0.08 + 0.08^2 + \dots + 0.08^N)
  \]

- **Cost Per Claim**: Assume \$20,000 annually.
- **Trust Fund Balance**: Start with an initial balance, say \( B \).
- **Depletion Model**: Each year, the trust fund decreases by the number of claims multiplied by \$20,000.

### 4. Municipal Bond Downgrade Pressure
- **Impact**: Fiscal strain leads to bond downgrades, increasing interest rates.
- **Assessment**: Model the likelihood of downgrades based on fiscal health indicators.
