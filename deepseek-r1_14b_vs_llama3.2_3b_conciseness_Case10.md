   - **Tax Loss**: With an average income tax rate of 10%, the state loses $40 million in revenue.

2. **Unemployment Insurance Trust Funds**:
   - **Increased Claims**: The 8% unemployment rate leads to a significant increase in unemployment claims, straining the trust fund.
   - **Revenue Decline**: Reduced employer taxes due to lower employment levels further depletes the trust fund, exacerbating the financial strain.

3. **Municipal Bonds**:
   - **Credit Rating**: A decline in fiscal health due to reduced revenues may lead to credit rating downgrades, increasing borrowing costs and investor skepticism.
   - **Bond Yields**: Higher yields may be required, affecting the state's ability to fund projects and services.

4. **Compounding Effect**:
   - **Suppressed Wage Growth**: AI-driven productivity gains suppress wage increases, leading to lower future tax revenues. This effect compounds over years, with subsequent annual contractions (e.g., 10%) causing greater revenue losses ($50 million in the second year).

5. **Fiscal Year Timeline**:
   - **Year 1**: 8% contraction, $40 million tax loss.
   - **Year 2**: 10% contraction, $50 million tax loss, accelerating trust fund depletion and bond strain.

6. **Structural Issues**:
   - **Consumer Spending**: Reduced spending impacts sales taxes and other revenues.
   - **Skills Mismatch**: Prolonged unemployment due to skills mismatch extends the economic downturn, further draining trust funds.

**Conclusion**:
The displacement of mid-skill white-collar workers by AI agents leads to an immediate $40 million tax loss, with subsequent years experiencing compounded revenue declines. The strain on unemployment insurance trust funds and municipal bonds, combined with suppressed wage growth, poses significant fiscal challenges for states and municipalities. Addressing these structural issues requires comprehensive strategies to mitigate economic impacts and ensure fiscal stability.
