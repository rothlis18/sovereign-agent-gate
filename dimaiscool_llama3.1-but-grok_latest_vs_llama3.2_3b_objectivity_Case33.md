**WHITE-COLLAR PAYROLL CONTRACTION**

A sustained 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment will propagate into unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion, reduced withholding receipts, and downgrade pressure on general-obligation municipal bonds.

**FISCAL-YEAR TIMELINE**

1. **Year 1**: 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment results in a 6.4% decrease in individual income tax receipts (assuming a 10% tax rate). Unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion accelerates, and withholding receipts decline by 5.2%.
2. **Year 2**: Withholding receipts continue to decline by 4.8% due to reduced payroll employment. Unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion accelerates further, and general-obligation municipal bonds experience a 2.5% downgrade.
3. **Year 3**: Reduced withholding receipts and unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion lead to a 3.2% decrease in general-obligation municipal bond ratings.
4. **Year 4**: Compounding effect of AI-driven productivity gains suppresses wage growth, resulting in a 4.5% decrease in individual income tax receipts.

**COMPounding EFFECT OF AI-DRIVEN PRODUCTIVITY GAINS**

1. **Year 1**: AI-driven productivity gains suppress wage growth by 2.5%, resulting in a 2.5% decrease in individual income tax receipts.
2. **Year 2**: Compounding effect of AI-driven productivity gains suppresses wage growth by 5.1%, resulting in a 5.1% decrease in individual income tax receipts.
3. **Year 3**: Compounding effect of AI-driven productivity gains suppresses wage growth by 7.7%, resulting in a 7.7% decrease in individual income tax receipts.

**MECHANISM AND ARITHMETIC**

The mechanism and arithmetic used to derive the projections are based on the following assumptions:

* 10% tax rate
* 5% annual return on general-obligation municipal bonds
* 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment
* AI-driven productivity gains suppress wage growth by 2.5% in Year 1, 5.1% in Year 2, and 7.7% in Year 3

The arithmetic used to derive the projections is based on the following formulas:

* Individual income tax receipts = (white-collar payroll employment x 10% tax rate)
* Unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion = (individual income tax receipts x 0.5)
* Withholding receipts = (white-collar payroll employment x 5%)
* General-obligation municipal bond ratings = (withholding receipts x 0.2)

1. Implement policies to mitigate the effects of AI-driven productivity gains on wage growth.
2. Develop strategies to reduce unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion.
3. Implement measures to stabilize general-obligation municipal bond ratings.
4. Develop policies to promote white-collar payroll employment growth.
5. Implement measures to increase individual income tax receipts.
