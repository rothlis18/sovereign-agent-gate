**Tax-Base Erosion Analysis: Autonomous AI Agents and Mid-Skill Knowledge-Economy Employment Displacement**

**Introduction**

The increasing adoption of autonomous AI agents in various industries is expected to displace mid-skill knowledge-economy employment at scale. This displacement will have a significant impact on municipal and state tax-base erosion, as individual income tax receipts fund a substantial portion of state general funds. This analysis will model the fiscal-year timeline over which a sustained 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment propagates into unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion, reduced withholding receipts, and downgrade pressure on general-obligation municipal bonds.

**Assumptions**

* Average annual wage growth rate: 3%
* Average annual tax rate: 10%
* Unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion rate: 5%
* General-obligation municipal bond downgrade pressure: 2%
* Autonomous AI agent adoption rate: 10% per annum
* Sustained 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment

**Fiscal-Year Timeline**

Year 1:

* White-collar payroll employment: 100,000 ( baseline)
* Unemployment-insurance trust-fund balance: $10,000,000
* Withholding receipts: $1,000,000
* General-obligation municipal bond rating: AAA

Year 2:

* Autonomous AI agent adoption rate: 10% (10,000 new jobs displaced)
* White-collar payroll employment: 90,000 (8% contraction)
* Unemployment-insurance trust-fund balance: $9,500,000 (5% depletion)
* Withholding receipts: $950,000 (5% reduction)
* General-obligation municipal bond rating: AA

Year 3:

* Autonomous AI agent adoption rate: 20% (20,000 new jobs displaced)
* White-collar payroll employment: 80,000 (16% contraction)
* Unemployment-insurance trust-fund balance: $8,500,000 (15% depletion)
* Withholding receipts: $900,000 (10% reduction)
* General-obligation municipal bond rating: BBB

Year 4:

* Autonomous AI agent adoption rate: 30% (30,000 new jobs displaced)
* White-collar payroll employment: 70,000 (25% contraction)
* Unemployment-insurance trust-fund balance: $7,000,000 (30% depletion)
* Withholding receipts: $850,000 (15% reduction)
* General-obligation municipal bond rating: BB

**Compounding Effect**

The compounding effect of AI-driven productivity gains simultaneously suppressing wage growth used to project future receipts can be modeled as follows:

* Year 1: 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment, 3% average annual wage growth rate
	+ Withholding receipts: $950,000 (5% reduction)
	+ Unemployment-insurance trust-fund balance: $9,500,000 (5% depletion)
* Year 2: 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment, 2% average annual wage growth rate (due to AI-driven productivity gains)
	+ Withholding receipts: $900,000 (10% reduction)
	+ Unemployment-insurance trust-fund balance: $8,500,000 (15% depletion)
* Year 3: 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment, 1.5% average annual wage growth rate (due to AI-driven productivity gains)
	+ Withholding receipts: $850,000 (15% reduction)
	+ Unemployment-insurance trust-fund balance: $7,000,000 (30% depletion)

**Conclusion**

The sustained 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment due to autonomous AI agent adoption will propagate into unemployment-insurance trust-fund depletion, reduced withholding receipts, and downgrade pressure on general-obligation municipal bonds. The compounding effect of AI-driven productivity gains simultaneously suppressing wage growth used to project future receipts will exacerbate these effects. Municipal and state tax-base erosion will increase, potentially leading to reduced government services and increased taxes.
