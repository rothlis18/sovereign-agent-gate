**Systems-Engineering Analysis of AI-Driven Employment Displacement Impact on Tax Bases**

**1. Immediate Tax Revenue Reduction (Year 0-1):**
   - **Employment Contraction:** An 8% reduction in mid-skill white-collar employment directly decreases income tax receipts by 8% from this sector.
   - **State Budget Impact:** Given that income taxes fund 40-50% of state general funds, the state's budget experiences a 3.2-4% reduction (8% * 40-50%).

**2. Unemployment Insurance Trust Fund Depletion (Year 1-2):**
   - **Increased Claims:** The 8% employment contraction results in an 8% increase in unemployment claims, stressing the trust fund.
   - **Trust Fund Solvency:** Without increased contributions, the trust fund depletes within two years due to the surge in claims.

**3. Reduced Withholding Receipts (Year 2-3):**
   - **Wage Suppression:** AI-driven productivity gains suppress wage growth by 2% annually.
   - **Cumulative Impact:** Over three years, this results in a 6.12% cumulative reduction in tax receipts from wages (2% annually compounded).

**4. Municipal Bond Downgrade and Increased Borrowing Costs (Year 3-4):**
   - **Fiscal Stress:** Reduced tax revenues and trust fund depletion lead to fiscal stress, prompting credit rating downgrades.
   - **Borrowing Costs:** Downgrades increase interest rates on municipal bonds, raising borrowing costs and straining municipal budgets.

**Conclusion:**
The displacement of mid-skill jobs by AI triggers a cascading fiscal crisis, impacting tax revenues, unemployment trust funds, and municipal bond ratings. Proactive measures are essential to mitigate these impacts and ensure fiscal stability.
