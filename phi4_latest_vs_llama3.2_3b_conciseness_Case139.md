**Analysis of Tax-Base Erosion Due to AI-Driven Displacement of Mid-Skill Employment**

**Initial Impact on Tax Receipts:**
- **Timeline:** Immediate to 1 year
- **Mechanism:** Autonomous AI agents displace mid-skill jobs, leading to an 8% contraction in white-collar payroll employment.
- **Effect:** Direct reduction in individual income tax receipts, which fund 40-50% of state general funds.
- **Arithmetic:** If state general funds are $100 billion, a 40% reliance on income tax implies $40 billion. An 8% job contraction reduces taxable income, potentially decreasing receipts by up to $3.2 billion (8% of $40 billion).

**Unemployment-Insurance Trust-Fund Depletion:**
- **Timeline:** 1 to 2 years
- **Mechanism:** Increased unemployment claims strain trust funds.
- **Effect:** Trust funds deplete faster due to higher payouts and reduced contributions from fewer employed individuals.
- **Arithmetic:** If trust funds receive $5 billion annually from payroll taxes, an 8% employment drop reduces contributions by $400 million, while increased claims further strain the fund.

**Reduced Withholding Receipts:**
- **Timeline:** 2 to 3 years
- **Mechanism:** Continued job displacement and wage suppression reduce withholding receipts.
- **Effect:** Further erosion of state revenue streams.
- **Arithmetic:** Withholding receipts decrease proportionally to employment contraction and wage suppression, compounding the initial $3.2 billion loss.

**Downgrade Pressure on Municipal Bonds:**
- **Timeline:** 3 to 5 years
- **Mechanism:** Reduced fiscal capacity and increased debt servicing costs.
- **Effect:** Credit rating agencies may downgrade municipal bonds, increasing borrowing costs.
- **Arithmetic:** If a city issues $1 billion in bonds at 3% interest, a downgrade to 4% increases annual interest payments by $10 million.

**Compounding Effect of AI-Driven Productivity Gains:**
- **Timeline:** Ongoing
- **Mechanism:** Productivity gains suppress wage growth, reducing future tax receipts.
- **Effect:** Long-term suppression of income growth exacerbates revenue shortfalls.
- **Arithmetic:** If wage growth is suppressed by 2%, and average taxable income is $50,000, the reduction in taxable income per capita is $1,000, further reducing tax receipts.

**Overall Fiscal Impact:**
- **Score for Each Transition:**
  - Initial Tax Receipt Reduction: -3.2 billion
  - Trust-Fund Depletion: -400 million annually
  - Withholding Receipts Reduction: -3.2 billion compounded
  - Bond Downgrade Pressure: +10 million in interest annually
  - Wage Growth Suppression: -1,000 per capita annually

**Conclusion:**
The displacement of mid-skill jobs by AI agents leads to a cascading fiscal impact, starting with immediate tax receipt reductions and progressing to long-term revenue shortfalls and increased borrowing costs. The compounding effect of suppressed wage growth further exacerbates the fiscal challenges faced by municipal and state governments.
