- **Assumption**: A sustained 8% contraction in mid-skill knowledge-economy employment.
- **Impact**: Immediate reduction in payroll, leading to lower individual income tax receipts.
- **Calculation**: If the initial payroll is $X$, an 8% reduction results in a payroll of $0.92X$.

**2. Transition to Unemployment Insurance**

- **Assumption**: 50% of displaced workers qualify for unemployment insurance.
- **Impact**: Increased demand on unemployment insurance trust funds.
- **Calculation**: 50% of $0.08X$ (displaced workers) = $0.04X$ in additional unemployment claims.

**3. Reduction in Withholding Receipts**

- **Impact**: Lower payroll leads to reduced withholding tax collected by the government.
- **Calculation**: Withholding tax is typically 15-20% of payroll. For the reduced payroll of $0.92X$, withholding receipts decrease to $0.138X$ to $0.184X$.

**4. Compounding Effect on Wage Growth**

- **Assumption**: AI-driven productivity gains suppress wage growth by 2% annually.
- **Impact**: Future payroll projections are lower, further reducing tax receipts.
- **Calculation**: If the initial payroll is $X$, after one year with 2% suppressed wage growth, the payroll becomes $0.98X$. After two years, it becomes $0.9604X$, and so on.

**6. Downgrade Pressure on Municipal Bonds**

- **Impact**: Reduced tax receipts and increased trust fund demands lead to credit rating downgrades.
- **Mechanism**: Lower tax revenues and higher unemployment claims strain the financial health of municipalities, reducing their creditworthiness.
- **Calculation**: Each year, the cumulative deficit in the trust fund increases, leading to a higher likelihood of bond downgrades. The exact timeline depends on the bond rating agency's criteria and the municipality's financial reserves.

**Conclusion**

The compounded effects of AI-driven employment displacement lead to a significant erosion of the tax base, with immediate impacts on unemployment insurance trust funds and long-term implications for municipal bond ratings. The arithmetic progression of payroll reductions and the suppression of wage growth exacerbate these effects, creating a fiscal crisis scenario that requires proactive policy interventions to mitigate.
